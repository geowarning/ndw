# Local rescue directory research — 14 November 2026

83 additions: 25 China, 41 New Zealand, 17 England/Wales. These are listed
organizations, not a claim that every team is currently available for dispatch.

- **Xiamen Shuguang:** Fujian Department of Veterans Affairs, report dated
  5 September 2026: https://tyjrswt.fujian.gov.cn/xwzx/sxdt/202609/t20260907_7209836.htm
  Documents boat evacuation, drainage and drone work. No direct contact number
  verified; do not substitute the department's switchboard or a guessed website.
- **Changsha Shuguang:** Changsha Evening News reporting republished by Ifeng,
  5 October 2026: https://feng.ifeng.com/c/8wyloX4P2ok
  Documents patrols, missing-person assistance and event first aid. This is a
  separate team from Xiamen; direct public contact remains unverified.
- **22 Qingdao organizations:** Qingdao government recognition notice,
  22 January 2024:
  http://www.qingdao.gov.cn/zwgk/xxgk/yjj/gkml/gwfg/202401/t20240126_7828817.shtml
  Includes Blue Sky, Zebra, Sunshine, Red Cross and other social emergency groups.
  This historical notice establishes listed identities and localities, not current
  operational status. Its government enquiry number is not a team dispatch number.
- **Yangdong Blue Sky:** Yangdong government reporting, 2026:
  http://www.yangdong.gov.cn/zzzq/ydyw/content/post_961208.html
  Documents emergency standby and first-aid support. Direct contact unverified.
- **41 local NZ groups:** LandSAR's official contact directory:
  https://www.landsar.org.nz/contact-us/
  Names and localities copied from the directory's group cards; only single
  identifiable town/city localities were included. North/South Island are source
  directory groupings, not administrative provinces. The official enquiry form
  routes non-emergency enquiries to a selected group. Within NZ, emergency SAR
  activation is through 111, ask for Police; the site gives separate guidance
  for international enquiries.
- **17 UK teams:** Mountain Rescue England and Wales's official team directory:
  https://www.mountain.rescue.org.uk/teams/
  Localities are the towns named in the teams. The published directory is the
  contact entry point; no unverified team phone or staging-site URL was copied.
